This ICLS paper develops and evaluates machine learning models that predict students' cognitive load from multimodal neurophysiological signals collected in real-world classrooms. The results show that physiological data can serve as reliable predictors of load states, supporting future work on real-time adaptive instruction and load-aware learning analytics.
