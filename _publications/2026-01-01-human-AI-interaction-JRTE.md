---Adopting cognitive distribution and regulation perspectives, this paper analyzes interaction patterns in human-AI collaborative problem solving. Using large-scale collaboration data, it identifies distinct interaction profiles and strategies, revealing how learners distribute cognitive work with AI and regulate the collaboration. The work contributes to understanding productive human-AI partnerships in education and was awarded the Best Paper Award at AERA 2026.
