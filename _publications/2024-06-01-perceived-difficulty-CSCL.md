---This CSCL paper proposes a novel interaction interface that allows learners to continuously annotate perceived difficulty while watching instructional videos, overcoming the intrusiveness of traditional experience sampling. The interface is evaluated for usability and data quality, laying groundwork for large-scale, fine-grained difficulty tracking in video-based learning environments.
