About Me
======
I am a Ph.D. student at the **School of Education, Tsinghua University**, under the supervision of [Prof. Yu Zhang](https://www.ioe.tsinghua.edu.cn/info/1131/3622.htm). I received my Bachelor's degree from the Department of Chemistry, Tsinghua University. 

My research focuses on **multimodal learning analytics** and its applications in **AI-supported education**. Specifically, I use multimodal neurophysiological data (EEG, physiological signals, etc.) to understand the implicit dynamic mechanisms underlying learning processes, and I examine how artificial intelligence reshapes learning contexts and educational practices. My recent work investigates human-AI collaboration in learning, AI instructional agents, and large-scale analysis of learner agency using large language models.

Research Interests
======
* Multimodal learning analytics
* AI-supported learning and human-AI collaboration
* Cognitive load and engagement in real-classroom learning
* Large language models for learning sciences (AI4LS)
* Educational data science
